Word riddles and letter puzzles

Twelve riddles that play with letters and spellings. They are good practice for English, and they work best when read aloud.

  1. Which five-letter word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it?

    Show answer

    Short. Add "er" and it becomes "shorter".

  2. What begins with P, ends with E and has thousands of letters in it?

    Show answer

    A post office

  3. I carry letters all day, yet I cannot spell a single word with them. Who am I?

    Show answer

    A postman

  4. Which word reads the same forwards, backwards and upside down?

    Show answer

    NOON

  5. Forwards I am heavy. Backwards I am not. Which word am I?

    Show answer

    Ton. Backwards it reads "not".

  6. Which letter of the alphabet holds the most water?

    Show answer

    C. It sounds like "sea".

  7. Which letter of the alphabet is a hot drink?

    Show answer

    T. It sounds like "tea".

  8. Which English word has three double letters one after another?

    Show answer

    Bookkeeper: oo, kk, ee.

  9. What is in the middle of India?

    Show answer

    The letter D

  10. I am a polite name for a lady. Read me backwards and I am still the same. Which word am I?

    Show answer

    Madam

  11. What begins with E, ends with E and holds only one letter?

    Show answer

    An envelope

  12. What comes at the end of everything?

    Show answer

    The letter G
