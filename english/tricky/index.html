Tricky riddles that fool almost everyone

Twelve riddles with a twist. The answer is simple once you hear it, which is exactly why people groan. Send one to your group and wait.

  1. Rekha's mother has four children. The first three are named East, West and North. What is the name of the fourth child?

    Show answer

    Rekha

  2. What can you hold in your right hand, but never in your left hand?

    Show answer

    Your left elbow

  3. Before Mount Everest was measured, which was the highest mountain in the world?

    Show answer

    Mount Everest. It was the highest even before anyone measured it.

  4. A plane crashes exactly on the border of two countries. In which country are the survivors buried?

    Show answer

    Nowhere. Survivors are not buried.

  5. Which word in the dictionary is spelled incorrectly?

    Show answer

    The word "incorrectly".

  6. Raju rode into a village on Sunday, stayed for three nights and rode out on Sunday. How?

    Show answer

    His horse is named Sunday.

  7. What goes up and never comes down?

    Show answer

    Your age

  8. I am full of holes, but I can still hold water. What am I?

    Show answer

    A sponge

  9. What begins with T, ends with T and has T in it?

    Show answer

    A teapot

  10. A boy fell off a ladder with twenty steps, but he was not hurt at all. How?

    Show answer

    He fell off the bottom step.

  11. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

    Show answer

    The letter M

  12. If you have me, you want to share me. Once you share me, you no longer have me. What am I?

    Show answer

    A secret
