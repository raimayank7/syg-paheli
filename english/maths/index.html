Maths riddles with answers

Twelve number riddles that need more thinking than calculating. Check each answer yourself: every one of them really works.

  1. I am an odd number. Take away one letter and I become even. Which number am I?

    Show answer

    Seven. Take away the "s" and you get "even".

  2. One grandmother, two mothers and two daughters sat down for tea. What is the smallest number of people at the table?

    Show answer

    Three: a grandmother, her daughter and her granddaughter.

  3. How many times can you subtract 5 from 25?

    Show answer

    Only once. After that you are subtracting from 20.

  4. A farmer has 17 goats. All but 9 run away. How many goats does he have left?

    Show answer

    Nine. "All but 9" means that 9 stayed.

  5. Double me and add 6, and you get 20. Which number am I?

    Show answer

    7. Twice 7 is 14, and 14 plus 6 is 20.

  6. Which is heavier: a kilogram of feathers or a kilogram of stones?

    Show answer

    Neither. Both weigh one kilogram.

  7. I had 6 eggs. I broke 2, cooked 2 and ate 2. How many eggs are left?

    Show answer

    Four. The two I broke, cooked and ate were the same two eggs.

  8. Water lilies in a pond double every day. On day 30 they cover the whole pond. On which day did they cover half of it?

    Show answer

    Day 29. They double the next day to cover all of it.

  9. When my father was 31, I was 8. Now he is twice as old as I am. How old am I?

    Show answer

    23. He is always 23 years older, so he is twice my age when I am 23 and he is 46.

  10. Using only addition, how can you make 1,000 with eight 8s?

    Show answer

    888 + 88 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 1,000.

  11. Which three whole numbers give the same answer when you add them and when you multiply them?

    Show answer

    1, 2 and 3. Added or multiplied, they make 6.

  12. A rope has to be cut into 6 equal pieces. How many cuts are needed?

    Show answer

    Five cuts.
